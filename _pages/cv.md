Research profile
======
Digital humanities graduate researcher examining the political and historical use of erasure language, moral panic, and anti-trans rhetoric. Combines comparative historical and discourse analysis with archival documentation, digital communication, and public-facing research education relevant to 2SLGBTQIA+ communities.

Education
======
* **M.A. Digital Humanities**, University of Alberta, September 2025 – Present (Expected August 2027)
  * Working thesis: *Moral Panics: The Code of Erasure Language* (proposal revisions submitted November 2026).
  * Comparative focus: Nazi Germany; Anita Bryant's Save Our Children campaign (1977–1980); and U.S. anti-trans political rhetoric and policy (2025–2026).
  * Planned primary research phase: January 2027; target thesis defense: August 2027.
* **B.A. Communications**, MacEwan University, 2024
  * Major: Journalism; Minor: Professional Communications.

Research interests and methods
======
2SLGBTQIA+ history and historical erasure; anti-trans political rhetoric; propaganda and moral panic; dehumanization and threat construction; archival preservation; critical discourse analysis; comparative historical research; digital text analysis (including Python-assisted n-grams, collocations, and TF-IDF) using programs like AntConc, as well as data-viz DH methods utilising programs like R and Gephi.
